HOW TO GET YOUR EXPORT

  1. On GitHub.com, open your enterprise or organisation.
  2. Go to Insights → Copilot usage.
  3. Select Export JSON, then download every part-*.ndjson or part-*.json file from that export.
  4. Select all of those files above.

Access requires the Copilot usage-metrics policy and an eligible enterprise or organisation role. Read GitHub’s export guidance ↗
